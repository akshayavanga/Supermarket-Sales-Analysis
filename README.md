# Supermarket Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes supermarket sales data using Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

The analysis focuses on sales performance, products, branches, categories, payment methods, customer types, and customer ratings.

## Objectives

- Analyze total sales and transactions
- Identify high-performing products
- Compare sales across branches
- Compare sales across product categories
- Analyze payment methods
- Compare Member and Normal customers
- Analyze customer ratings
- Create visualizations
- Provide business recommendations

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset

The dataset contains 500 supermarket sales transactions with information about:

- Invoice ID
- Date
- Branch
- City
- Customer Type
- Gender
- Product
- Category
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Payment
- Rating
- Sales

## Key Results

- Total Sales: ₹244,411.08
- Total Transactions: 500
- Total Quantity Sold: 2,768
- Average Sales per Transaction: ₹488.82
- Average Customer Rating: 3.99/5

## Analysis Performed

The project includes:

## Business Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing products such as Cheese, Coffee, and Shampoo.
- Study the successful practices of Branch C.
- Promote the highest-selling category, Beverages.
- Encourage UPI and Net Banking payments.
- Improve customer experience to maintain strong ratings.
- Analyze Normal customers because their average transaction value is slightly higher than Member customers.
- Use customer ratings to identify areas for product and service improvement.
